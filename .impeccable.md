# .impeccable

Persistent design context for **CAOS — Credit Agent OS**. This file is the
source of truth for design decisions across all sessions. The visual
vocabulary below is already established in code
([globals.css](caos/frontend/app/globals.css)); the principles govern how to
extend it.

## Design Context

### Users

The product is an **institutional leveraged-finance credit analysis platform**
(high-yield / loan universe), surfaced through eight destinations plus one
tool: Portfolio, Credit, Sources, Analysis with its tool Run, Market, Model,
Report, and Admin (an unavailable-capability surface), one word each in the
URL, the rail, the kicker and the tab title (`DESIGN.md` 2026-09-06 addendum).
The rail groups them as seven workflows (Portfolio, Credit, Sources, Analysis,
Market, Model, Report) plus Governance / Admin, and the Run tool renders under
"Analysis tools" on every surface. Analysts can use six pathways: Full Credit, Earnings Update,
Covenant & Refinancing, Relative Value, Distressed & Restructuring, and
source-bound Deep Research. Three personas use it, but **when trade-offs force
a choice, optimize for the buy-side credit analyst** — the person doing the
deep work.

- **Buy-side credit analyst (primary).** Lives in Analysis, Model, and
  Report. Context: building a defensible credit view under time
  pressure, moving between source documents, the model, and the narrative.
  Job-to-be-done: reach a conclusion they can stand behind in front of an
  investment committee, with every claim traceable to evidence.
- **PM / CIO (secondary).** Scans Credit for portfolio posture and
  "what changed." Needs material risk at a glance and the freshest signal first.
- **Head of Research / QA (secondary).** Owns coverage health, the CP-5 QA gate,
  and governance — completeness, freshness SLAs, audit trail.

Their shared context: dense, multi-window, numbers-heavy work where a wrong read
has real money behind it. They are specialists; they value precision over
hand-holding and will not tolerate decoration that costs them information.

### Brand Personality

**Three words: precise, defensible, alert.**

The interface should make the analyst feel four things at once — and they are
not in tension, they are layers:

- **Calm institutional authority.** Output looks committee-ready. No drama, no
  noise, nothing that reads as a consumer app. Restraint is the default.
- **Trading-desk alertness.** Live state, freshness, and "what changed" feel
  immediate. Pulse and motion are reserved for things that are genuinely live.
- **Confident clarity.** Dense complexity is made legible. The user feels on top
  of a large amount of information, never buried by it.
- **Trust through transparency.** Every number is one click from its source.
  Evidence → risk mechanic → credit implication is always reachable. The UI
  earns trust by showing its work, never by asserting.

Voice/tone in copy: terse, technical, exact. Label like a desk, not a brochure
(`Net leverage`, `3Y DM +504bps`, `pack HELD`). No marketing language, no
exclamation, no emoji in product chrome.

### Aesthetic Direction

**A refined institutional terminal — a *designed* Bloomberg, not a raw one.**
Keep the dense, dark credit-desk feel, but make every pixel intentional. The
current code already trends here; the job is to hold that bar, not to soften
toward generic SaaS and not to degrade into raw utilitarian clutter.

**Established visual system (inherit, don't reinvent):** the modern dark terminal
adopted on 31 August 2026 at the product owner's instruction (graphite ramp, iris
accent, retuned semantics, a 6–14px radius scale, faint resting panel shadows, the
native display face for chrome headings only, severity-shaped status glyphs). It
replaced the earlier blue accent, all-square geometry and flat panels, none of which
survive in `globals.css` or in this file.

- **Theme: dark, single mode for the workspace.** Surfaces ramp from
  `--caos-bg #0a0c10` → `--caos-panel #101319` → `--caos-elevated #181d28` →
  `--caos-subtle #202632`, hairline `--caos-border #242b38`, control border
  `--caos-border-strong #606b7e`, text `--caos-text #e9edf4`, muted
  `--caos-muted #99a3b4`, iris accent `--caos-accent #8b93f8` with
  `--caos-accent-strong #a5abfa` for text on elevated surfaces. Radii run
  6/8/10/14px (`--radius-sm` … `--radius-xl`) with `--radius-pill` for chips and
  status dots; a resting panel carries `--shadow-panel`, dialogs and the context
  drawer `--shadow-modal`, the report paper `--shadow-paper`; the 3px dialog
  backdrop blur is the one sanctioned blur.
- **Semantic color is signal, never decoration:** warning `#fbbf24`,
  critical `#f87171`, success `#34d399`; idle uses `--caos-muted`. Color earns
  its place only when it carries meaning.
- **Seniority is text** in the loan table; no tranche colour ramp exists in the
  code. Do not draw one until a token lands in `globals.css`.
- **Typography:** native system stacks (`--font-sans`, `--font-display`,
  `--font-mono`; no web font is shipped). Numerics use `.num` (`tabular-nums`)
  or `.mono`. Labels are 9–11px sans, uppercase and tracked only for table
  heads, rail group labels, worksheet tabs and report rail labels. The 46px
  sentence-case `.panel-header` (13px/600) is the structural unit of the whole
  app.
- **Motion:** 160ms `--ease-out` transitions on hover and focus channels; a
  240ms width transition on the live progress bar; a 1.6s shimmer on loading
  skeletons inside `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`. No pulse, no enter
  animation, no dialog entrance motion. Always honor `prefers-reduced-motion`
  (a global `.01ms` pin on every transition and animation, `.app-shell` paused).
- **Output aesthetic (Report) is a deliberate counterpoint:** a light
  "paper" tear-sheet — ink `--caos-ink #191922` on `--caos-paper #f7f4ec`, mono
  mastheads, hairline paper rules, print-ready. Reports look like a document an
  institution would file, not a screenshot of the app.
- **Filed output typography is explicit:** paper titles 21px/650, section
  heads 11px/700 uppercase, body 12px/400/1.62, mono metadata 8.5px/500, table
  labels 9px/650 uppercase. The full-model appendix scale lives in the worker's
  PDF/XLSX renderers, not in the web CSS. This scale is valid only inside
  Report, paper/print roots, and research paper deliverables; it must
  not leak into workspace chrome or analytical UI.

**Anti-references — what this must NOT become:**

- Not a friendly consumer SaaS dashboard (oversized type, pastel cards, rounded
  everything, generous empty space, illustrative spot art).
- Not a raw, unstyled terminal dump — density without hierarchy is clutter, not
  craft. Density must always be *organized*.
- No gradients-as-decoration, no glow, no skeuomorphism, no stock iconography.

### Accessibility & Inclusion

**Target: WCAG 2.1 AA, and colorblind-safe.**

- Body and label text meets **4.5:1** contrast against its surface; large/bold
  text meets 3:1. Measured (FE-A2 §7): muted on panel is 7.31:1; the lowest
  enabled text pair is critical on elevated at 6.10:1; disabled primary buttons
  read 2.96:1 (exempt from 1.4.3, recorded as F-04).
- **Status meaning is never carried by color alone.** Pair every semantic color
  with a non-color cue: a glyph/shape, a text label, or position. Every
  `.status` tone draws a distinct glyph shape: success and running a disc,
  warning a triangle, critical a rounded square, idle a flat dot.
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` for every animation (already wired — keep it
  that way for anything new).
- Interactive surfaces are keyboard-reachable with a visible focus ring; the
  evidence-chip to context-drawer link (`.evidence-chip.is-linked`) is operable
  without a mouse, and under `pointer: coarse` every control takes a 44px
  minimum height.

### Design Principles

1. **Density with hierarchy.** More on screen is good *only* when the eye knows
   where to go. Earn density with clear grouping, the panel idiom, and
   consistent rhythm — never with raw cramming.
2. **Color is signal, not decoration.** Reserve hue for status, seniority, and
   selection. A surface that adds color without adding meaning is a regression.
3. **Show your work.** Every conclusion stays one interaction from its evidence.
   Traceability is a feature of the UI, not a footnote — design for the click
   that proves the number.
 4. **Motion only for life.** Animate what is genuinely live or changing (running
    modules, fresh alerts, selection). Decorative motion erodes the calm
    authority the product depends on; always degrade gracefully under
    reduced-motion.
 5. **Committee-ready by default.** When in doubt, choose the option that would
    survive scrutiny in front of an investment committee: precise labels,
    defensible numbers, restrained styling. Polish means *intentional*, not
    *ornamented*.
